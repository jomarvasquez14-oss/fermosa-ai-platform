**No selector was changed and the registry stays at `crm-selectors/v1`** — the honest
consequence of never seeing the live DOM. Bumping or "fixing" selectors without live
evidence would be guesswork, which ADR-033 forbids.

## Phase 6 — the validation cockpit (`/dev/browser`)

The page now has two halves:

- **Framework drills** (unchanged): mock-driver session/navigation/failure simulation.
- **CRM connector cockpit** (new): everything goes through `getCRMConnector()` —
  credential-presence badges (`CRM_URL` / `CRM_USERNAME` / `CRM_PASSWORD`, presence
  only, never values), connector health probe, **patient search** (name / mobile /
  CRM ID), **open patient**, and the **normalized JSON preview** of the full
  `NormalizedCrmPatientRecord`. Stat cards show connector kind + readiness, login
  status, session state, and selector registry version.

With `CRM_CONNECTOR=mock` (today) the cockpit drives the mock connector. The moment
`CRM_CONNECTOR=playwright` + the three credentials land in `.env`, the SAME buttons
execute Phases 1–5's checklist against the live CRM under human supervision
(`CRM_BROWSER_HEADLESS=false` recommended for the first run). That is the point of
ADR-034: live validation is a supervised activity with a purpose-built cockpit, not a
headless batch job.
